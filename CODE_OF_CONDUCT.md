# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the person you are replying to is trying to help.

## What is expected

- Criticise the code, not the person who wrote it.
- Accept that "no" is a legitimate answer, and that a maintainer who declines your idea is
  not dismissing you.
- Say what you mean plainly. Directness is fine; contempt is not.
- Remember that most people here are working in a second language, on their own time.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discrimination or demeaning comments about who someone is
- Sexual language or imagery
- Publishing someone's private information
- Sustained disruption — reopening settled arguments, brigading a thread

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere else someone is
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainer by email. Reports stay private.

Responses, depending on severity: a private word, a public correction, having a comment
removed, a temporary ban, or a permanent one. The maintainer decides, and will say what was
decided and why.

If the person you need to report is the maintainer, say so publicly in an issue. A project
where the only complaint channel runs through the person you are complaining about is not a
channel.
